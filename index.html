About

Hello! My name is Youngji. I am a PhD student in Economics at the University of Warwick.

I work in applied microeconomic theory and game theory. I am curious about how people act on beliefs about aspects of others they cannot fully observe.

I am on the 2026/27 academic job market.

My letter writers are Daniele Condorelli, Herakles Polemarchakis, and Debraj Ray.
